The article introduces a framework for dashboard design based on three questions: context (what to show), audience (who it is for), and insight (what should change). This approach centers on user decision-making rather than data display.

In a project for a B2B SaaS platform for enterprise talent management, applying this context-first approach helped the team filter out 90% of noise and identify a checkout bottleneck. This illustrates the potential concrete benefits of the method in a real product environment.

It is important to note that the article is based on author opinion and anecdotal examples rather than measured outcomes or user studies. Therefore, the effectiveness of the framework and case study lacks rigorous validation.
