AI systems are nondeterministic: the same input can produce different outputs across runs. Evaluating AI with a single output is therefore insufficient; proper evaluation uses multiple representative inputs and repeated runs to capture the distribution of outputs.

For design and product teams, this means that assessing AI features (such as generative interfaces or content summaries) must account for variability. Drawing conclusions from just one or two examples can lead to poor product decisions. Instead, report summary statistics like average performance and confidence intervals to get a more accurate picture of real-world performance.
