AI lets teams build faster than UX can evaluate, leading to UX debt when teams haven’t taken time to understand, evaluate, or refine AI output. UX can adapt by building shared judgment, accelerating evaluation, and adding UX guidance to AI generation tools.

Teams adopting AI-generated workflows are affected: they need to work in a “custodial pattern” that includes new ideas, rapid production, lagging UX evaluation, user-experience problems, and UX cleanup.
