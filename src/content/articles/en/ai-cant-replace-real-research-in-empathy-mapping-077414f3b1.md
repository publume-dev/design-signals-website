According to the Nielsen Norman Group article, legitimate uses of AI in empathy mapping include organizing and synthesizing user research data, not generating user research evidence. AI-generated content may seem plausible but lacks the specificity and evidential value of real user quotes.

For UX designers and product teams, this means AI can only assist in creating empathy maps, not replace real research. Teams can use AI to organize sticky notes, clean up language, summarize patterns, and draft first-pass groupings, but must review and correct the output.

Empathy mapping is a visualization tool that helps teams understand users' needs and feelings. The use of AI in this context is still emerging, and the article does not provide quantitative data or measured outcomes on its effectiveness.
